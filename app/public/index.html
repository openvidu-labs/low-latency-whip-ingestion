low-latency-whip-ingestion

A companion demo for the OpenVidu blog's Low Latency Live Streaming series: push a live stream into an OpenVidu Room over WHIP and watch it come out the other side, sub-second, end to end.

📷

Publish from your webcam

A browser page that captures your camera and mic, and publishes them straight to OpenVidu over WHIP -- no plugin, no separate encoder.
📺

Watch the stream

Joins the room as a subscriber and renders whatever is currently being published, from either the browser publisher or OBS.

🎥 Publish with OBS instead

OBS Studio has had a built-in WHIP output since version 30. Generate a one-time set of credentials below, then paste them into Settings → Stream → Service: WHIP. Full walkthrough in the README.
